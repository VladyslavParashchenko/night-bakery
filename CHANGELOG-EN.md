# What's new

## 1.3.1 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Played cards fly into the oven: yours from your hand or wherever you let
  go of it, and your opponents' from their seat at the table, so their moves
  are easier to follow.

## 1.3.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Drag a card from your hand straight onto an oven. On a touch screen,
  hold the card for a moment first. Picking a card and then an oven still
  works.

## 1.2.0 — 31 October 2026

### Changed

- You win the moment you run out of cards instead of at the end of your
  turn. The winning card can no longer be called out with "Scorched!".

## 1.1.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Play with friends online: create a room and send your friends the code or
  link. Bots can fill the empty seats and take over for a player who leaves.

## 1.0.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Night Bakery against 1–4 bots.
- Full rules in the menu and right at the table.
- Ukrainian and English, light and dark themes.
- Install it as an app and play against bots offline.
