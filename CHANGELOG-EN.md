# What's new

## 1.9.1 — 7 November 2026

### Fixed

- Text on the table is no longer highlighted when selected, so taps and
  drags don’t paint it blue.

## 1.9.0 — 7 November 2026

### Added

- Table setup: the “Set up the table” button in the menu and in an online
  room lets you pick 3 to 6 ovens and the card that burns an oven, from the
  3rd to the 8th. The dialog shows what the table will look like. The
  choice is remembered and a rematch keeps it. Guests see the host's table
  in the room, and the host is warned if someone's version is too old to
  show it. The standard rules still use three ovens that burn at the fifth
  card.

## 1.8.0 — 7 November 2026

### Changed

- Ovens look like real ones: cards bake in a dark arched chamber, the
  flames beneath rise higher with every card, and the marks below are now
  embers. When an oven is one card away from burning, the last ember
  flickers.

## 1.7.1 — 4 November 2026

### Changed

- The rules show the cook icons: 🧹 🔒 🕵️ 🎠 👨‍🍳.
- The start page has less space at the top, so the menu fits on short
  screens.

## 1.7.0 — 4 November 2026

### Added

- Scoring at the end of the game. Every card left in hand is worth penalty
  points: a pastry its value, a cook 15, a Chef 20. Places now go by
  points, and with three or more players whoever has the most points is
  marked as the worst baker. The points table is in the rules.
- Bots mind the points: they shed cooks and Chefs when an opponent is about
  to win, and are keener to play high pastries.

## 1.6.1 — 2 November 2026

### Changed

- On phones, opponents' seats are more compact and sit two to a row, so more
  of the table fits on screen.
- The theme button now has three settings: same as system, light and
  dark. Before, a single press stopped the game from following the
  system theme, so dark mode could fail to turn on.

## 1.6.0 — 1 November 2026

### Changed

- A new end-of-game window. It shows whether you won or which place you
  took, with medals, plus how many turns the game lasted, how many ovens
  burnt and how long it took. You can also see the cards each player was
  left with. Rematches keep a series score.
- A win is greeted with a shower of flour, and the result rows appear one by
  one. There is no animation if reduced motion is on in your system.
- "View the table" hides the results so you can look at the final position.
- In games with friends, guests can press "Play again" too, and everyone sees
  who wants a rematch.
- The game opens in Ukrainian whenever Ukrainian or Russian is among your
  browser's languages, even below English. Otherwise it now opens in English
  rather than Ukrainian.

## 1.5.0 — 31 October 2026

### Changed

- On large screens the whole game is bigger: it grows with the window as far
  as the table still fits in its height. The end turn button now sits beside
  your hand there.
- On Android, the installed game's icon now fills the whole shape instead of
  sitting small inside a white circle.

## 1.4.1 — 31 October 2026

### Changed

- The menu button for online play now has a simple icon.
- New game icon featuring the night baker, ovens, and all four suits.

### Fixed

- Playing with friends no longer hangs on "Connecting…" when the online
  part of the game fails to load; an error is shown instead.

## 1.4.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Sounds for a card landing in an oven, drawing, a burnt oven, "Scorched!",
  your turn, winning and losing. Turn them off with the speaker button in the
  menu or at the table.

## 1.3.1 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Played cards fly into the oven: yours from your hand or wherever you let
  go of it, and your opponents' from their seat at the table, so their moves
  are easier to follow.

## 1.3.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Drag a card from your hand straight onto an oven. On a touch screen,
  hold the card for a moment first. Picking a card and then an oven still
  works.

## 1.2.0 — 31 October 2026

### Changed

- You win the moment you run out of cards instead of at the end of your
  turn. The winning card can no longer be called out with "Scorched!".

## 1.1.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Play with friends online: create a room and send your friends the code or
  link. Bots can fill the empty seats and take over for a player who leaves.

## 1.0.0 — 31 October 2026

### Added

- Night Bakery against 1–4 bots.
- Full rules in the menu and right at the table.
- Ukrainian and English, light and dark themes.
- Install it as an app and play against bots offline.
